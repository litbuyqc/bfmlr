A short guide to what this page provides and how to use a shopping research list.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, categories, measurements, prices, and notes. A list helps compare options; it does not verify a seller or guarantee a product.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This page is an independent research guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS categories or the CAFANS homepage.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a practical structure for building your own shortlist in a spreadsheet.

How should I compare sizes across listings?

Record measurements in centimeters, compare the same measurement points, and use a garment that fits you as a reference. A size label alone is not a reliable comparison.

Can a product photo confirm authenticity?

No. A product photo or brand name alone cannot confirm authenticity. Check the seller, provenance, and applicable purchase protections before making a decision.
